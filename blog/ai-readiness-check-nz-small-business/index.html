What is an AI Readiness Check — and what you get from ours

Most NZ business owners spend months wondering where to start with AI. An AI Readiness Check cuts that to a week. Here's exactly what the process looks like — and what lands in your hands at the end.

17 May 2026 5 min read

There's a specific kind of frustration that NZ business owners describe when they talk about AI. It's not confusion about the technology. It's not scepticism about whether it works. It's this: they don't know where they sit.

They know AI is doing real things for real businesses. They know their competitors are probably moving on it. But without a clear picture of their own business — which processes are ready to automate, what tools they already have, where the biggest wins are — every decision about AI feels like a guess.

That's exactly what an AI Readiness Check is designed to solve.

What an AI Readiness Check actually is

It's a structured audit of your business through an AI lens. Not a sales call. Not a general "here's what AI can do" presentation. A systematic look at how your business runs today — your workflows, your tools, your team, your data — and a clear assessment of where AI would make a real difference.

The output isn't a generic report. It's a scored assessment of your specific situation, with the quick wins prioritised and a written recommendation of what to do first.

If you're still at the "should we even bother?" stage, start with where to start with AI if you run a small NZ business first. The Readiness Check is for businesses that are past that — they know they want to move, they just need to know where.

The five dimensions we assess

Our assessment covers five areas that consistently determine how ready a business is — and how much ROI they'll see from AI in the first 12 months.

  • Workflows and processes. Which tasks are repetitive, rule-based, and high-volume? These are the highest-value automation targets. We map the ones eating the most time.
  • Existing tools and systems. What software does your business already run? The best AI integrations work with what you have — not around it. We assess what's connectable.
  • Team capacity and skills. Where is your team's time actually going? We look for the gap between what your people are trained for and what they're spending time on — that gap is usually where AI has the most impact.
  • Data and information flows. AI works best when it has good inputs. We look at how information moves through your business — what's captured, what's lost, what's entered twice.
  • Growth bottlenecks. What's slowing you down right now? Leads that don't get followed up fast enough. Quotes that take too long. Onboarding that's inconsistent. We identify which of these AI can remove.

What you get back

At the end of the check, you receive a branded AI Readiness Score report. It's written for a business owner — not a tech team — and it's specific to your business, not a template with your name on it.

The report includes:

  • Your AI Readiness Score across each of the five dimensions
  • 3–5 prioritised quick wins — specific opportunities, ranked by ROI and ease of implementation
  • A plain-English explanation of what each opportunity involves and what it'd take to set up
  • A recommended first step — the one thing worth doing next, with rough cost and timeline

We then book a 30-minute walkthrough call to go through the findings with you. You can ask questions, challenge the priorities, or dig deeper on anything in the report. By the end of that call, you'll know exactly where your business stands and what to do about it.

Delivered within one week

From the discovery call to the walkthrough, the whole process takes about one week. Your total time commitment: roughly one hour — 30 minutes at the start, 30 minutes at the end. We do the work in between.

Who it's designed for

The AI Readiness Check is built for NZ businesses that are past curiosity and into "right, let's actually figure this out." You don't need to be technical. You don't need a dedicated IT person. You just need to be running a business with real processes that could work better.

It works well for trades and service businesses, professional services, retail and hospitality operations, and any team that's grown to the point where manual processes are starting to hold them back.

If you've already ticked three or more of the readiness signals in our earlier post — 5 signs your NZ business is ready for AI — a Readiness Check is the logical next step.
