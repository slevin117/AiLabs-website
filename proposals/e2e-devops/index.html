Background

Where e2e is headed

e2e is on an AI journey — building internal and client-facing tools that connect into SimPro. The team is moving fast using AI-assisted development, and the next step is making sure the foundations underneath that work are solid, secure, and scalable.

Confidentiality: Everything you share with AiLabs — code, systems, credentials, business context — is treated as strictly confidential. It will not be shared with, stored by, or referenced outside of this engagement. Full confidentiality terms apply from first contact.

Internal tools Reporting and quoting tools used by the e2e team, integrated with SimPro via API
Client-facing tools Products for e2e's clients — e.g. solar panel quoting tool that connects into each client's SimPro end-to-end
Development approach AI-assisted development — fast to build, but needs proper process & workflow to scale safely
The challenge Git is in use but without the right tools, process, and workflow to support a growing, client-facing product

Discovery & Advisory
Fixed Price

A focused review of your current setup followed by a written recommendations document — so you have a clear, prioritised plan before any implementation begins. Part of this session will also identify which areas e2e wants to own and maintain vs hand off to AiLabs.

Fixed  ·  No GST Delivered within 5 business days of discovery session. Non-refundable once session occurs.

Discovery includes

GitHub & Development Workflow We'll review how the team currently uses Git, identify the gaps in branching, PR process, and commit workflow, and recommend exactly what needs to change.
Build & Release Automation We'll assess what happens between a code change and it going live — and identify where automation can reduce manual steps and risk, especially for client-facing tools.
AI Workflow & Skill Implementation We'll review how AI tooling is currently used in development and identify where better habits, safer practices, and smarter workflow patterns would improve quality as the team scales.
Hosting, Scaling & Integration We'll assess your current hosting setup and recommend the right platforms for each workload — covering how they'll scale, integrate, and be managed as usage grows.
Two-environment Architecture We'll review whether your internal and client-facing tools are structured to scale independently — and recommend an architecture that lets both grow without conflict.

Deliverables

Discovery session — 1–2 hr video call, review current codebase & setup
Written recommendations document — prioritised plan covering all areas above, delivered as PDF
Walkthrough call — 30-min session to walk through the document and answer questions
Implementation roadmap — what to set up, in what order, with effort estimates for Phases 2 & 3
